<h1 id="party-creation-and-chat" data-toc-id="party-creation-and-chat">Party creation and chat</h1>
<p><a href="?file=&quot;Learning Path&quot;">Learning path and videos</a> · <a href="?file=&quot;Troubleshooting and Data&quot;">Troubleshooting</a></p>
<p>Goal: create Village Explorers, coordinate departure and keep party friendly fire off. Ordinary players do not need OP; the UI command requires permission level 0.</p>
<pre language="plaintext" isclosed="false"><code class="language-plaintext">/viscript_team party
/viscript_team party open</code></pre>
<ol><li><p>Click Create Party and enter a trimmed name of 1–32 characters. The creator becomes leader and the system generates the party ID.</p>
</li><li><p>Check the role and actions on the left. The displayed name differs from the fixed ID used by admin commands.</p>
</li><li><p>Enter a message on the right, then Send or Enter. It is party-only and refreshes online members’ screens.</p>
</li><li><p>Open Party Settings to inspect friendly fire. Toggling applies immediately and closes the dialog; it is off by default.</p>
</li></ol>

<h2 id="chat-retention" data-toc-id="chat-retention">Chat retention</h2>
<p>Messages allow up to 256 characters. Each party keeps its last 100 messages for the current server session. Parties and membership are saved, but chat is cleared by server restart or disbanding.</p>
<h2 id="what-party-friendly-fire-affects" data-toc-id="what-party-friendly-fire-affects">What party friendly fire affects</h2>
<p>It controls damage permission between players in the same party. Faction friendly fire is a separate setting. Vanilla PVP, other mods and the damage source may apply additional rules.</p>
<h2 id="success-check" data-toc-id="success-check">Success check</h2>
<p>Check that leader actions appear, your message is shown and reopening settings preserves the chosen state. Continue with membership management to add teammates.</p>
