<h1 id="viscriptteam-user-guide" data-toc-id="viscriptteam-user-guide">ViScriptTeam User Guide</h1>
<p>ViScriptTeam (VST) provides player parties, entity factions and player reputation. Players form parties, chat and manage membership through the UI; map authors use the faction editor, commands and scripts for guards, rival factions and reputation gates.</p>
<p><a href="?file=&quot;Learning Path&quot;">Start with the learning path: four recordings and complete scenarios</a></p>

<h2 id="how-the-three-systems-fit-together" data-toc-id="how-the-three-systems-fit-together">How the three systems fit together</h2>
<table data-type="custom-table" data-with-header-row="true"><tbody><tr><th>System</th><th>Stores</th><th>Effect</th></tr><tr><td>Player party</td><td>Members, leader, party friendly fire, invitations and requests</td><td>Defines membership and available actions; does not assign players to an entity faction.</td></tr><tr><td>Entity faction</td><td>Fixed ID, display name, representative color, enemies and faction friendly fire</td><td>Assigns factions to guards, mobs and NPCs; hostility is directional.</td></tr><tr><td>Player reputation</td><td>An integer for each player/faction pair</td><td>Becomes friendly, neutral or hostile; built-in party evaluation uses the lowest member value.</td></tr></tbody></table>
<h2 id="installation-and-first-check" data-toc-id="installation-and-first-check">Installation and first check</h2>
<p>Install VST and its required LDLib2 dependency on matching client and server environments. ViScriptLib is embedded in the release; KubeJS is needed for the scripting examples. Follow the dependency declarations of the downloaded files.</p>
<ol><li><p>Join a world and run /viscript_team party to see your solo state.</p>
</li><li><p>As an admin, run /viscript_team faction to open the faction editor.</p>
</li><li><p>Complete a party or guard scenario before adding quests and other integrations.</p>
</li></ol>
