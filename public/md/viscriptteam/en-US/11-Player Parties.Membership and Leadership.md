<h2 id="requests-and-invitations-are-separate-routes" data-toc-id="requests-and-invitations-are-separate-routes">Requests and invitations are separate routes</h2>
<ol><li><p>Request route: a solo player chooses Browse Parties -> Apply; the leader uses Join Requests -> Accept. Applying alone does not join.</p>
</li><li><p>Invitation route: the leader selects an online solo player under Invite Player; the invitee accepts under Invitations.</p>
</li><li><p>Invite candidates exclude yourself, grouped players, existing invitations and requests. Search accepts name/UUID fragments; check these conditions when someone is absent.</p>
</li><li><p>Existing requests/invitations are shown as status. If a player joins another party first, stale requests are cleaned up.</p>
</li></ol>

<h2 id="right-click-member-management-and-leadership" data-toc-id="right-click-member-management-and-leadership">Right-click member management and leadership</h2>
<ol><li><p>As leader, right-click another member and choose Kick or Transfer; the dialog identifies the member.</p>
</li><li><p>After transfer you become a regular member and the target becomes leader. You can then use Leave Party.</p>
</li><li><p>Leaders cannot leave through the player UI. Disband a solo party or transfer first. Admin forced-leave commands choose a remaining leader.</p>
</li></ol>

<h2 id="offline-members-and-persistence" data-toc-id="offline-members-and-persistence">Offline members and persistence</h2>
<p>Offline players remain members and requests/invitations are saved. The list marks online/offline state; normal invite candidates are online only. Effective reputation includes valid members, so a low-reputation member going offline does not raise the minimum.</p>
<div type="warning" data-type="admonition" data-admo-type="warning"><div data-type="admonition-title">Disbanding</div><div data-type="admonition-content"><p>Disbanding removes the whole party and clears its session chat. Admin force-joining another party also removes the old membership.</p>
</div></div>
