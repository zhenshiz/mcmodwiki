<h2 id="how-reputation-becomes-attitude" data-toc-id="how-reputation-becomes-attitude">How reputation becomes attitude</h2>
<table data-type="custom-table" data-with-header-row="true"><tbody><tr><th>Default points</th><th>Attitude</th><th>Behavior</th></tr><tr><td>&lt; 50</td><td>Hostile: red</td><td>Faction mobs may target the player; no friendly protection.</td></tr><tr><td>50–99</td><td>Neutral: yellow</td><td>No reputation-based proactive targeting; neutrality alone does not block damage.</td></tr><tr><td>&gt;= 100</td><td>Friendly: green</td><td>Relevant player/faction-entity damage is protected when faction friendly fire is off.</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>These are default thresholds for new factions. Existing factions store their own thresholds. Unset player reputation uses that faction’s default, normally 50.</p>
<h2 id="whose-reputation-counts-in-a-party" data-toc-id="whose-reputation-counts-in-a-party">Whose reputation counts in a party</h2>
<p>Parties do not store a separate reputation balance. Evaluation reads valid members’ individual standings, including offline members. For a leader at 100 and a member at -50:</p>
<table data-type="custom-table" data-with-header-row="true"><tbody><tr><th>Strategy</th><th>Result</th><th>Use</th></tr><tr><td>min</td><td>-50</td><td>Built-in targeting, damage and name-tag default.</td></tr><tr><td>average</td><td>25</td><td>Average rounded down; useful for script scoring.</td></tr><tr><td>leader</td><td>100</td><td>Leader only; for deliberately leader-based story logic.</td></tr><tr><td>max</td><td>100</td><td>Maximum; for scripted any-member gates.</td></tr></tbody></table>
<pre language="plaintext" isclosed="false"><code class="language-plaintext">/viscript_team party standing get &lt;party&gt; tutorial_guards
/viscript_team party standing get &lt;party&gt; tutorial_guards average
/viscript_team party standing get &lt;party&gt; tutorial_guards leader</code></pre>
<p>Replace &lt;party&gt; with the fixed ID from party list/get. An explicit strategy changes that query only, not the server’s built-in default.</p>

<h2 id="targeting-and-attack-ability" data-toc-id="targeting-and-attack-ability">Targeting and attack ability</h2>
<p>VST adds low-priority targeting to the standard Mob target system. Players use effective reputation; assigned non-player entities use the attacker’s enemy list and targeting switch. Unassigned mobs do not perform extra faction scans. Hostility does not give a non-combat mob an attack ability, and custom mod AI may behave differently.</p>
<h2 id="name-tags-and-representative-color" data-toc-id="name-tags-and-representative-color">Name tags and representative color</h2>
<p>Only name tags that would already render receive attitude colors for the viewing player. Use CustomName/CustomNameVisible as needed. Representative color neither forces visibility nor makes a friendly entity red.</p>
