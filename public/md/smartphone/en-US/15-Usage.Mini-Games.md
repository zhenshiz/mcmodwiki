<h1 id="phone-mini-games" data-toc-id="phone-mini-games">Phone Mini-Games</h1>
<p>Open the App Store on the phone home screen, install the mini-games you want, and enter them from their home screen icons. The mini-games are Snake, 2048, Flappy Bird, Minesweeper, and Piano Tiles. The bottom return bar inside an app returns to the phone home screen; after a game ends, the Restart button is available.</p>
<p>Held phone, the regular phone interface, and the phone editor preview all use the same game canvas. Mouse clicks are mapped to cells according to the scaled canvas position; changes to the phone interface size do not alter game rules.</p>

<h2 id="piano-tiles" data-toc-id="piano-tiles">Piano Tiles</h2>
<p>When the game starts, there is one green tile at the bottom. Left-click it to begin scoring, after which the black tiles move downward. Keep clicking the current lowest black tile to continue scoring; clicking a higher black tile early neither ends the game nor awards points.</p>
<p>Clicking a white tile fails immediately, and the mistaken white position turns red. If you do not click the current black tile in time, it stops at the bottom and turns red once its <strong>bottom edge touches the board's bottom line</strong>, ending the game. Click Restart to replay from the green start tile.</p>

<h2 id="minesweeper" data-toc-id="minesweeper">Minesweeper</h2>
<p>The board is 6 columns by 15 rows with 15 mines. Left-click any unmarked cell to start; the first clicked cell and its neighbors never contain mines. Numbers indicate how many mines are in the eight surrounding cells, and blank cells automatically expand into adjacent safe areas.</p>
<p>Right-click an unrevealed cell to place a flag and right-click again to remove it; the remaining flag count is shown at the top. Revealing a mine loses the game; revealing every non-mine cell wins. The board is tall, so you can scroll on it to view lower cells. After the game ends, click Restart to reset the board.</p>
