<h3 id="how-to-read-ranges" data-toc-id="how-to-read-ranges">How to read ranges</h3>
<p><code>[a,b)</code> includes a and excludes b; <code>[a,)</code> means at least a. These are VSR optional dependency declarations. An absent upper bound does not replace testing newer versions. No declared bounds does not mean every version is compatible; use the build dependency as the troubleshooting reference.</p>
<p>Mekanism retains its full build number here; the mod list may show 10.7.19. Create uses the slim development dependency 6.0.10-280. Ice and Fire refers to the CE fork and Bakery to its Farm & Charm branch. Additional addon dependencies are on their individual pages.</p>
