<h2 id="configuration-and-defaults" data-toc-id="configuration-and-defaults">Configuration and defaults</h2>
<p>The file is config/viscript_team_config.toml, also available through NeoForge’s mod configuration screen. Defaults:</p>
<pre language="toml" isclosed="false"><code class="language-toml">[faction]
defaultFactionPoints = 50
neutralFactionPoints = 50
friendlyFactionPoints = 100
defaultFactionFriendlyFire = false
defaultAttackEnemyFactions = true</code></pre>
<table data-type="custom-table" data-with-header-row="true"><tbody><tr><th>Key</th><th>Meaning for new factions</th></tr><tr><td>defaultFactionPoints</td><td>Fallback for unset player standing.</td></tr><tr><td>neutralFactionPoints</td><td>Hostile below this threshold; at least neutral when reached.</td></tr><tr><td>friendlyFactionPoints</td><td>Friendly at or above this threshold.</td></tr><tr><td>defaultFactionFriendlyFire</td><td>Initial faction friendly fire, not party friendly fire.</td></tr><tr><td>defaultAttackEnemyFactions</td><td>Whether to seek entities in the enemy list.</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Keep the neutral threshold at or below the friendly threshold. These five defaults do not rewrite existing factions. Edit existing friendly-fire and targeting flags in the editor.</p>
<h2 id="backup-scope" data-toc-id="backup-scope">Backup scope</h2>
<p>Parties, factions, entity UUID assignments, standings, invitations and requests share overworld SavedData across dimensions, normally data/viscript_team_factions.dat. Configuration and session chat are separate; preserve the whole world and config during migration.</p>
